The Curry library
=================

The Curry library under ``curry/lib`` is the ``base`` library of PAKCS,
with Sprite's own modules beside it.  It carries the license of PAKCS and
a notice of its origin.  The distribution also carries the notices of
pybind11 (BSD 3-Clause) and libtommath (public domain), which the runtime
links.
